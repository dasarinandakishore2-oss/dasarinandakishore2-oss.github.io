NCC and NSS in RGUKT: Complete Guide for Students (Nuzvid, RK Valley, Ongole, Srikakulam)

12 min read · RGUKT Life · Back to all guides

Advertisement

If you're a student at any RGUKT campus — Nuzvid, RK Valley, Ongole, or Srikakulam — you've probably heard about NCC and NSS. Both are government-run programs available to college students across India, and both offer real benefits: certificates that help in government job applications, career advantages in defence and civil services, and activities that genuinely add value beyond academics. Here's everything you need to know, explained clearly.

What are NCC and NSS?

🎖️ NCC

National Cadet Corps (NCC)

Founded: 1948 | Ministry: Ministry of Defence, Government of India
Motto: "Unity and Discipline"

NCC is India's largest voluntary youth organization under the Armed Forces. It provides military-style training to school and college students — drill, weapon handling, map reading, first aid, physical fitness, and leadership. It is not compulsory. Students voluntarily enroll and train for 2–3 years to earn NCC certificates (A, B, C). NCC is divided into three wings: Army, Navy, and Air Force.

🤝 NSS

National Service Scheme (NSS)

Founded: 1969 | Ministry: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India
Motto: "Not Me, But You"

NSS is a government program that promotes community service, social awareness, and civic responsibility among students. Unlike NCC, NSS focuses on social work — blood donation camps, cleanliness drives, awareness programs, village development projects, and disaster relief. Students complete 240 hours of service over two years to earn the NSS certificate. All RGUKT campuses have active NSS units.

NCC at RGUKT — Campus-wise Details

🏫 RGUKT Nuzvid (IIIT Nuzvid) Krishna District

RGUKT Nuzvid has an active NCC unit under the Army wing. NCC is available as an extracurricular activity for B.Tech students. The unit is affiliated with the AP & Telangana NCC Directorate. Students can enroll in the first year and train for the 'B' and 'C' certificates over the course of their program.

Contact: Director's office, RGUKT Nuzvid | director@rguktn.ac.in | 08656-235147

NSS at RGUKT — What Students Actually Do

NSS at all RGUKT campuses runs activities throughout the year. Here's what participation typically involves:

  • Regular activities (throughout the year) — 2 hours per week of community service, awareness programs, and social projects near campus
  • Special camp (7 days) — Mandatory once a year, usually in a nearby village. Activities include building projects, health camps, adult literacy, and cleanliness drives
  • Blood donation camps — Organized on campus, often in coordination with district hospitals
  • Swachh Bharat activities — Cleanliness and sanitation awareness in nearby villages
  • Disaster preparedness — First aid training and awareness programs
NSS Completion requirement: Students must complete 240 hours of NSS service over 2 years — including the 7-day special camp — to receive the NSS certificate. The certificate is issued by the university and recognized by the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports.

Benefits of NCC — Why It Matters

🪖

Defence Services

NCC 'C' certificate holders get direct entry and reserved vacancies in Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. Bonus marks in CDS exam.

👮

Paramilitary & Police

Preference and bonus marks in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, Coast Guard, IPS, CAPF, and state police recruitment.

🎓

Higher Education

NCC quota seats in many universities and colleges. Some IITs and NITs reserve seats for NCC 'C' certificate holders.

✈️

International Exposure

Youth Exchange Programme — selected NCC cadets visit other countries. Foreign cruise opportunities for Navy wing cadets.

🏔️

Adventure Activities

Mountaineering, trekking, water skiing, scuba diving, sailing expeditions — free for selected NCC cadets.

💰

Scholarships

One-time NCC scholarship: ₹6,000 (Class 10 level) and ₹12,000 (Class 12 level) for eligible cadets.

Benefits of NSS

📜

NSS Certificate

Recognized certificate from Ministry of Youth Affairs. Adds weight to resume for government jobs and higher education applications.

🏆

National Awards

Outstanding NSS volunteers receive National NSS Awards presented by the President of India — a prestigious recognition.

🌍

Youth Parliament

Selected NSS students get to participate in Youth Parliament programs and national integration camps across India.

💼

Resume Value

NSS participation demonstrates community commitment — valued by employers in government, NGOs, and corporate CSR roles.

Important for RGUKT students: NCC and NSS activities happen outside regular class hours. Make sure participation doesn't affect your attendance percentage. If you're attending NCC/NSS camps, carry the official permission letter from the activity officer — most RGUKT departments count authorized NCC/NSS absences separately from regular attendance.

How to join NCC or NSS at your RGUKT campus

  1. First week of academic year — Both NCC and NSS conduct enrollment drives at the start of the semester. Watch for notices on your campus notice board.
  2. Contact the NCC/NSS officer — Each campus has a designated faculty member as NCC officer and NSS Program Officer. Ask your class teacher or check with the administration.
  3. Fill the enrollment form — Basic details, medical fitness declaration, and parent consent (for some activities).
  4. Attend the orientation — First session explains the year's schedule, commitments, and activities.
  5. Stay consistent — Both NCC and NSS require regular participation. Irregular attendance means no certificate at the end.
The NCC 'C' Certificate is one of the most underrated advantages you can earn during B.Tech — it opens doors in defence, government jobs, and higher education that marks alone cannot.

Other government organizations at RGUKT

Beyond NCC and NSS, RGUKT campuses also have:

  • Youth Red Cross (YRC) — Blood donation, first aid, and health awareness activities
  • AICTE-sponsored student chapters — Technical clubs with government backing
  • Rotaract Club — Community service linked to Rotary International
  • Student Government / Senate — Campus-level elected student body managing campus welfare
  • SC/ST Cell — Government-mandated cell supporting reserved category students with scholarships and welfare
  • Women's Cell — Addresses issues specific to female students, mandatory under UGC guidelines
  • Anti-Ragging Committee — Government-mandated under UGC regulations — all RGUKT campuses maintain this
