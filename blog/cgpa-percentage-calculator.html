When you apply for jobs, internships, or higher education, most applications ask for a percentage — but your marksheet shows a CGPA. Here's the formula and a live calculator to convert instantly.

JNTU CGPA to Percentage Formula

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

This is the official formula used by JNTU Hyderabad for converting CGPA to equivalent percentage for the purpose of job applications and further studies.

How to use this for job applications

Most companies accept either format — CGPA or percentage — as long as you specify which scale it's on. When filling application forms:

  • If they ask for percentage: use the formula above and write "X% (converted from CGPA Y.Y on JNTU 10-point scale)"
  • If they ask for CGPA: write your CGPA directly
  • Never round up — write the exact figure your transcript shows

For campus placements, HR teams are familiar with JNTU's grading system and will typically do the conversion themselves. For off-campus applications, it helps to include the formula or a note so there's no confusion.

Does CGPA or percentage matter more for placements?

Most companies that recruit from engineering colleges set cutoffs in terms of CGPA — commonly 6.5 or 7.0 on a 10-point scale. Some older companies and PSUs still specify percentage cutoffs (60% or 65%). Either way, the number on your transcript is the one that matters — the conversion is just for communication on forms.
