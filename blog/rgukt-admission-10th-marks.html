How to Join RGUKT Colleges in AP Based on 10th Marks (2026 Complete Guide)

12 min read · Guides · Back to all guides

Advertisement

RGUKT (Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies) is one of the best opportunities for students from rural backgrounds in Andhra Pradesh — a free 6-year integrated B.Tech program based purely on 10th class marks, with free hostel and food. This guide explains the entire process clearly, step by step.

What is RGUKT?
RGUKT runs 4 campuses in AP offering a 6-year integrated program — 2 years Pre-University Course (PUC, equivalent to Intermediate) + 4 years B.Tech. Admission is based only on SSC (10th class) marks. No entrance exam required.

AP students apply to the 4 AP campuses: RK Valley, Nuzvid, Ongole, and Srikakulam. Each campus has its own seat intake and slightly different cutoffs depending on demand.

Who is eligible to apply?

  • Must have passed SSC (10th class) from a recognized board (AP Board, CBSE, ICSE)
  • Must be an AP domicile student (studied in AP for at least 4 years before 10th)
  • Age: should not have completed 17 years as of 31st December of the admission year (varies slightly by year — check official notification)
  • No minimum percentage is officially specified, but practically you need 85%+ to have a realistic chance in general category
  • Must not have studied Intermediate (11th/12th) — RGUKT PUC replaces Intermediate
Important: If you have already joined Intermediate (Junior college) after 10th, you are NOT eligible for RGUKT admission. Apply only if you haven't joined any course after 10th.

Reservation categories

RGUKT follows AP government reservation rules:

  • SC — 15%
  • ST — 6%
  • BC-A, B, C, D, E — 29% (combined)
  • EWS — 10%
  • General/OC — 40%

Cutoffs are significantly lower for SC/ST and BC categories — even 75–80% in 10th can be sufficient for reserved categories at some campuses.

Step-by-step application process

1
Check the official notification
Visit rgukt.ac.in (AP campuses) when results are announced. The notification is released within 1–2 weeks of SSC results. Note the application start and last date.
2
Register online
Go to the RGUKT admissions portal. Register with your mobile number and email. You'll receive an OTP to verify. Keep your mobile number active throughout the process.
3
Fill the application form
Enter your SSC details — Hall Ticket Number, school details, marks, category (OC/BC/SC/ST/EWS), local area status, and parent income. Double-check every field — errors here cause problems at counselling.
4
Upload documents
Required: SSC marks memo (scan or clear photo), caste certificate (if applicable), income certificate (for EWS), Aadhaar card, passport-size photo, and signature. All must be clearly legible.
5
Pay application fee
Application fee is typically ₹300–500 (varies by year). Pay online through the portal using UPI, net banking, or debit card. Keep the payment receipt.
6
Download and submit application
After submitting online, download the filled application PDF. Some years require submitting a hard copy at your nearest Mee Seva center or at the school — check the notification carefully.
7
Check rank/merit list
RGUKT releases a merit list based on SSC marks (percentage). Your rank determines which campus and branch you can get in counselling.
8
Attend counselling
Counselling is conducted online (web options) or in-person depending on the year. You will fill campus and branch preferences in order. Seats are allotted based on rank and availability.
9
Report to campus
After seat allotment, report to the campus with original documents within the specified date. Documents verified, fee paid (if any — most students get full scholarship), and admission confirmed.

Documents required at counselling

  • SSC (10th) Original Marks Memo
  • SSC Pass Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card (original + photocopy)
  • Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/BC — issued by MRO)
  • Income Certificate (for EWS — issued by MRO, must be current year)
  • Residence/Domicile Certificate or Study Certificate (showing AP study for 4+ years)
  • School Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Passport-size photos (carry 6–10)
  • Application printout
Scholarship at RGUKT: Most students admitted to RGUKT receive the Jagananna Vidya Deevena scholarship or similar state scholarship that covers the full tuition fee. Hostel and mess (food) are provided at subsidized rates. For students from families with income below ₹2.5 lakh per year, the program is essentially free.

Which campus should you choose?

If your marks are strong (90%+), you'll likely have a choice. Here's how to think about it:

  • RK Valley (Kadapa) — Oldest and most established campus. Strong alumni network. Slightly more competitive cutoff.
  • Nuzvid (Krishna) — Well-connected location, good infrastructure. Popular choice for students from Krishna and Guntur districts.
  • Ongole (Prakasam) — Good campus, slightly lower cutoff than Nuzvid. Good option if you're from South AP.
  • Srikakulam — Newest campus, lower cutoff. Good option if your marks are in the 85–88% range.

All four campuses offer the same 6-year B.Tech program with the same RGUKT degree. The branch you get matters more than the campus for long-term outcomes — Computer Science and ECE are the most in-demand branches at all campuses.

Branches available at RGUKT

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — highest demand, highest cutoff
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME)
  • Civil Engineering (CE)
  • Chemical Engineering (ChE) — at some campuses

What if your marks aren't enough for RGUKT?

If your 10th percentage is below the expected cutoff for your category, RGUKT may not be an option this year. In that case:

  • Join Intermediate (MPC — Maths, Physics, Chemistry) and appear for EAMCET after 12th — this opens all engineering colleges in AP/TS
  • Appear for EAMCET and look at state government engineering colleges — many are excellent and affordable
  • Consider polytechnic diploma programs if you want to start engineering-related work sooner
At RGUKT, branch matters more than campus. CSE at Srikakulam beats Civil at RK Valley for placements.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply if I studied in CBSE?

Yes. CBSE students are eligible as long as they have AP domicile (studied in AP schools). Your CBSE percentage is considered directly.

Is there a separate exam for RGUKT?

No. Admission is purely based on SSC (10th class) marks. There is no entrance exam.

Can girls apply?

Yes. RGUKT admits both male and female students. Separate hostels are provided for female students on all campuses.

What happens after 2 years PUC at RGUKT?

After successfully completing the 2-year PUC, students automatically move into the 4-year B.Tech program at the same campus. There is no separate entrance at this stage for RGUKT students.

Is the degree accepted by companies?

Yes. RGUKT is a central university established by an Act of Parliament. The B.Tech degree is recognized by UGC and accepted by all companies. RGUKT campuses have active placement cells — CSE and ECE branches in particular have strong placement records.
