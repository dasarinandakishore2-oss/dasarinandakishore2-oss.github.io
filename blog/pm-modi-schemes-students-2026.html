PM Modi Schemes for Students 2026: Complete List of Scholarships, Internships & Benefits

12 min read · Government Schemes · Back to all guides

Advertisement

The Government of India runs dozens of schemes specifically for students — scholarships, paid internships, skill training, and education loans. Most students don't apply simply because they don't know these schemes exist or think they won't qualify. This guide covers every major PM Modi government scheme for students in 2026, clearly explained with eligibility, benefits, and how to apply.

Quick tip: All government schemes can be searched at myscheme.gov.in — the official one-stop portal for all central government schemes. Enter your details and it shows which schemes you're eligible for.

1. Scholarships

Scholarship

PM Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)

💰 ₹2,500/month (Boys) | ₹3,000/month (Girls) 📅 Annual — 5,500 seats 🏛️ Ministry of Defence

Started in 2007, PMSS provides monthly scholarships to wards and widows of ex-servicemen, CAPF personnel, and Assam Rifles personnel. The scholarship is for professional degree courses — engineering, medicine, MBA, MCA, pharmacy, nursing, and more. Important: This is specifically for families of defence/paramilitary personnel — not for general students.

Eligibility: Ward or widow of ex-serviceman/CAPF/AR personnel. Must have 60%+ in 12th. Pursuing professional degree.

Apply at ksb.gov.in →
Scholarship

PM YASASVI Scholarship

💰 ₹75,000–₹1,25,000/year 🎯 OBC, EBC, DNT students 🏛️ Ministry of Social Justice

PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India (YASASVI) provides financial support to students from OBC (Other Backward Class), EBC (Economically Backward Class), and DNT (De-notified Tribes) categories. Class 9 and 11 students are selected on merit basis. Amount ranges from ₹75,000 to ₹1,25,000 per year depending on the level of study.

Eligibility: OBC/EBC/DNT category. Class 9–12 or college. Family income below ₹2.5 lakh per year.

Apply at yet.nta.ac.in →
Scholarship

National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Scholarships

💰 Various — ₹1,000 to ₹20,000/year 🎯 SC/ST/OBC/Minority/Girls 🏛️ Multiple Ministries

NSP is the single portal for all central government scholarships. Key scholarships available here include: Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarships for SC/ST students, Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for minorities, Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for college students (top 20 percentile in 12th board), and National Fellowship for ST students (up to ₹2.5 lakh for research).

Eligibility: Varies by scheme — mostly based on category (SC/ST/OBC/Minority) and income below ₹2.5 lakh/year.

Apply at scholarships.gov.in →

2. Internship Schemes

Internship

PM Internship Scheme 2026 ⭐ Most Important

💰 ₹9,000/month stipend 💰 ₹6,000 joining bonus 📅 12 months 🎯 Age 18–25

This is the biggest government scheme for students in 2026. The PM Internship Scheme places youth in top Indian companies — Reliance, Tata, HDFC, Infosys, and 490+ others — for 12 months of real paid work experience. The government pays ₹4,500/month directly to your bank account, and the company adds ₹4,500 more, totalling ₹9,000/month. Plus a one-time ₹6,000 joining bonus (₹3,000 on joining + ₹3,000 after 3 months). Insurance under PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima and PM Suraksha Bima is also provided free.

2026 updates: Age limit expanded to 18–25 years. Final-year UG and PG students can now apply (with NOC from college).

NOT eligible: IIT/IIM/NLU graduates, CA/CMA/MBBS holders, family income above ₹8 lakh/year, family member in government job.

Eligible courses: Class 10/12, ITI, Diploma, any UG or PG degree.

Apply at pminternship.mca.gov.in →

3. Education Loan Schemes

Education Loan

PM Vidya Laxmi Education Loan Portal

💰 Loans up to ₹7.5 lakh (no collateral) 💰 Interest subsidy for income below ₹4.5 LPA 🏛️ Ministry of Education + Finance

PM Vidya Laxmi is a single portal where students can compare and apply to multiple bank education loans at once — without visiting each bank separately. Students from families with annual income below ₹4.5 lakh get full interest subsidy under the Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) scheme, meaning the government pays your loan interest while you're studying. Loans up to ₹7.5 lakh require no collateral.

Eligibility: Indian student admitted to recognized college/university. Income below ₹4.5 lakh/year for interest subsidy.

Apply at vidyalakshmi.co.in →

4. Skill Development

Skill Training

Skill India — PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

💰 Free training + certificate 🎯 Youth 15–45 years 🏛️ Ministry of Skill Development

PMKVY provides free vocational training in 300+ job roles — IT, electronics, construction, hospitality, healthcare, and more. Training happens at Skill India centres across India. After completing the course, students get a government-recognized certificate that can be used for jobs. Some courses also include a stipend during training.

Eligibility: Any Indian citizen aged 15–45. No minimum qualification for most courses.

Apply at skillindiadigital.gov.in →

5. Higher Education Schemes

Higher Education

PM USHA — PM Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan

🏛️ Ministry of Education 🎯 State university students

PM USHA provides central government funding to state universities and colleges to improve quality — better labs, libraries, faculty, and infrastructure. It directly benefits students studying in government colleges by upgrading the quality of education. RGUKT receives benefits under similar central schemes as a central university.

Education Policy

New National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

🏛️ Ministry of Education 🎯 All students

NEP 2020 is the biggest education reform in India since 1986. Key changes for students: flexible degree structure (exit after 1, 2, or 3 years with certificate/diploma/degree), vocational training from Class 6, teaching in mother tongue up to Grade 5, and emphasis on critical thinking over rote learning. For engineering students, NEP encourages internships from the second year onward as part of the curriculum.

How to check which schemes you're eligible for

  1. Go to myscheme.gov.in
  2. Click "Find Schemes for Me"
  3. Enter your age, gender, category, state, and income
  4. Get a personalized list of all schemes you qualify for
  5. Click any scheme to see the apply link and full details
Most students miss out on government schemes simply because they don't search for them. 10 minutes on myscheme.gov.in can find money that's already been allocated for you.

Most important scheme for RGUKT/Engineering students right now

If you're an engineering student aged 18–25 and your family income is below ₹8 lakh/year, the PM Internship Scheme is the single most valuable opportunity available to you in 2026. ₹9,000/month for 12 months of real work at a top company — with government insurance — is hard to match. Applications open in rounds throughout the year at pminternship.mca.gov.in.
