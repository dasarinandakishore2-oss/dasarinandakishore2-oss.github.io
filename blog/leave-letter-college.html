How to Write a Leave Letter to College: Samples for Every Reason

5 min read · Guides · Back to all guides

Advertisement

A leave letter to college needs to be short, formal, and give a clear reason. Most departments accept a simple one-page letter addressed to the HOD or Principal. Here are ready-to-use samples you can copy and fill in your details.

Format to follow (every letter)

  • Date at the top right
  • Address to: The HOD / The Principal
  • Subject line
  • Body: your name, roll number, reason, dates
  • Close: "Thanking you" + your signature

Sample 1: Medical leave (fever/illness)

Date: ___________ To, The Head of Department, Department of [Your Branch], [Your College Name], [City]. Subject: Application for Medical Leave Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], a student of [Year] year, [Branch], Roll Number [Your Roll No.]. I am writing to inform you that I am suffering from fever and am unable to attend college from [Start Date] to [End Date]. I request you to kindly grant me leave for [Number] days. I will submit the medical certificate upon my return. Thanking you, Yours faithfully, [Your Name] [Roll Number] [Branch & Year]

Sample 2: Family function / wedding

Date: ___________ To, The Head of Department, Department of [Your Branch], [Your College Name]. Subject: Application for Leave to Attend Family Function Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], Roll No. [Your Roll No.], studying in [Year] year, [Branch]. I wish to inform you that there is a family wedding/function at my home on [Date(s)], and my presence is required. I kindly request you to grant me leave for [Number] day(s) from [Start Date] to [End Date]. I assure you that I will complete any missed work promptly upon my return. Thanking you, Yours faithfully, [Your Name] [Roll Number]
Advertisement

Sample 3: Personal reasons

Date: ___________ To, The Principal, [Your College Name]. Subject: Application for Leave on Personal Grounds Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], Roll No. [Your Roll No.], [Year] year, [Branch]. I am writing to request leave for [Number] day(s) from [Start Date] to [End Date] due to urgent personal reasons at home. I request you to kindly grant me this leave. I will ensure that I do not fall behind in my studies and will complete all pending work after rejoining. Thanking you, Yours faithfully, [Your Name] [Roll Number] [Contact Number]

Tips for a leave letter that gets approved

  • Submit before the absence if possible — letters submitted in advance are more likely to be accepted than ones submitted after.
  • Keep the reason honest and brief. Don't over-explain or invent dramatic reasons — a simple, clear reason is more credible.
  • Check your attendance before applying. If you're already below 75% in a subject, some departments won't grant leave without a medical certificate. Know your number before you ask.
  • Follow up with a medical certificate if the reason was illness — most departments require one for absences of two or more days.
