What's inside

Everything that actually eats a student's headspace, in one place.

Not a note-taking app repurposed for school. Scholar is built around the specific, recurring problems of attending college — the ones that quietly cost you marks if you lose track of them.

Attendance, tracked honestly

Mark present or absent per period. See your real percentage per subject, updated the moment you mark it — the number your department actually checks before exams.

A timetable that just works

Set your subjects once. Fixed period timings mean you're never re-entering start and end times for every single day.

Tasks with real deadlines

Add a start date and a deadline, not just a note. See what's due today the moment you open the app.

A calendar that remembers holidays

Mark a day off once and it's remembered. Sundays are handled automatically, so your attendance math never gets thrown off by a day you were never meant to attend.

A study timer with a daily goal

Start it when you sit down to study. It tracks toward a five-hour goal and keeps a quiet daily score, so effort stops being invisible.

Jarvis, an assistant that opens things

Ask for what you need in plain language — "open tasks," "start the timer" — or just talk to it. It runs on your own API key, so it costs you only what you use.

Why it exists

Most attendance problems aren't about attending. They're about not knowing the number until it's too late.

Colleges rarely tell you your attendance percentage until the week before exams — and by then, one more absence in a low subject can put you below the eligibility line. Scholar keeps that number visible every single day, so the decision to skip a class is one you make with the real cost in front of you, not after.
